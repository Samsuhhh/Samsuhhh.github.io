01About

I'm a front-end developer in Campbell, CA. For two years at Honest Digital I built and maintained 30+ client web properties across AEM, WordPress, Ansira, DealerInspire, DealerOn, Dealer.com, and Apollo. A lot of it was compliance-critical work, including FTC disclosure builds, PII handling, and WCAG accessibility compliance.

I care about the parts of front-end that don't demo well: accessible markup, cross-browser behavior, and components other people on the team can actually reuse. Lately, I've been building my own tools and assistants, and that's how CareerOS and SamOS were born.
